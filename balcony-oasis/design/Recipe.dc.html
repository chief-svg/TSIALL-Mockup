Balcony Oasis · appendix

How the renders were made

Kept here in case a view needs redoing. The finished images came from ChatGPT editing the original photos one change at a time, then a realism and 4K pass in Adobe Firefly with the Gemini Nano Banana Pro model.

Turning the plan into a photograph

These drawings are to scale but they are drawings. For a photo-real version of your own balcony, edit your photos in ChatGPT one small step at a time, in the order below, each step on the previous image. Asking for the whole design at once makes the model rebuild the balcony at the wrong size; one change per message keeps the real geometry. Sizes are given against things in the photo (the chair seat, the top cap) because that is what the model can actually see. For 4K, take the largest image the app produces and upscale it 2× to 4× with Upscayl, Topaz Gigapixel or Photoshop Super Resolution.
