Rainbow Gardens · balcony project · September 2026
Plants for a north-facing, roofed balcony: bright shade, no direct sun
For the staff: everything on this list is going into containers on a covered, north-facing 13.5 × 6 ft balcony that gets sky light but no direct sun. Please pull shade-tolerant, well-branched plants. Where a size is listed, it is the container size we need; if that size is out, the next size down is fine for the boxwood and jasmine, but the bay laurel and the two podocarpus need to be at least the size shown.

Plants

Plant type: shrub
Size: 3 gallon
Sunlight: partial shade
8 for a clipped hedge in two 48-inch troughs, 12 inches apart, plus 2 to clip as balls in 14-inch pots. Please match them for size and color.

Plant type: evergreen vine
